What the data tells us

01
Suite is where Campaigns monetizes
The 4x SEM conversion delta is not a messaging gap — it's a product-fit signal. Existing monday customers already have trust, contacts, and workflows in the platform. Campaigns is a natural extension. For first-time users, it has to win standalone against HubSpot, Mailchimp, and Brevo.
$1.6M suite ARR vs $60K standalone. 96% of Campaigns revenue lives inside the monday ecosystem.
02
Cross-sell is under-penetrated, not exhausted
1.6% WM attach rate and 7.6% CRM attach rate are not ceilings — they're starting points. WM x-ray shows 91K marketing-activity accounts, 13K email-marketing accounts, and 62K social-media accounts with zero Campaigns today. The opportunity is large and largely untouched.
Only 11 WM-Campaigns customers exist today. The runway is far longer than the current attach rate suggests.
03
Standalone has real demand — but thin economics
90% new users via SEM confirms genuine market awareness. The demand signal is real. But S-tier company skew, lower LTV, and 4x weaker pay conversion make the standalone ARR path long and expensive without significant product and positioning investment.
60–70% of all sign-ups are net-new to monday. Demand exists. The question is whether the economics can support a standalone acquisition motion at scale.
